How It Works

From discovery to activation in four steps.

1) Discover

Search and filter leads by firmographics, demographics, technographics, and location. Preview attributes and export samples.

2) Enrich

Upload existing records to append missing data and correct inaccuracies. Configure matching rules and output schemas.

3) Segment

Create audience segments using rules and look‑alike models. Score for intent and likelihood to convert.

4) Activate

Sync to CRM/MAP or launch campaigns with deliverability checks, personalization, and attribution tracking.

Industry Use Cases

B2B SaaS Lead Generation

Target ICP by industry, employee range, installed tech, and growth indicators. Route enriched leads to SDRs.

E‑commerce Segmentation

Build personas from purchase patterns and demographics. Personalize lifecycle messaging to increase LTV.

Nonprofit Fundraising

Identify high‑affinity donors with wealth and interest signals. Optimize outreach with channel preferences.

Financial Services Compliance

Verify and maintain KYC attributes with change detection and audit logs aligned to regulations.
