Under morning light, neighbors rolled out planters, painted curb stripes, and installed benches to convert a sleepy side street into a lively parklet. The pop‑up build brought together residents, shop owners, and students who wanted a greener, safer corner for gatherings.

## Why it matters

Small, community‑led projects can change how people use shared spaces. Benches invite conversation, plants cool hot pavement, and art signals that the street belongs to everyone.

<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1470093851219-69951fcbb533?w=1200&q=80" alt="Freshly painted bench with flowers" />

## What we learned

- Keep materials simple and durable.
- Assign clear roles (paint crew, planters, safety marshals).
- Invite passersby to paint a tile or plant a seedling.

## What’s next

Organizers are hosting a feedback walk next week and planning a care calendar so the space stays tidy. If you’d like to adopt a planter or propose a mini‑event, add your name to the sign‑up sheet.
